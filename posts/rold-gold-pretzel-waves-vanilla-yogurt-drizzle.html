Rold Gold Pretzel Waves, Vanilla Yogurt Drizzle

March 14, 2009


I love pretzels. I really love pretzels with sweet stuff on top.. Chocolates good, but Ive tried it so many ways – today, I tried Rold Gold Vanilla Yogurt Drizzle.. Delicious little pretzel chips that have this sort of krimped wave, and are drizzled with a yogurt tasting type topping. Yummy num nums.
