Always Infinity

November 03, 2008

I have to do another post, after such a long time.. because this is really good.. A whole new pad – that is completely different from anything on the market. Always Infinity has this really thin, kind of like a thinly sliced shamois (shammy) – they call it infinicel.. it sucks up all the yucky stuff and pulls it down to the bottom side of the pad.. so you are nice & dry.

Sorry, friends.. I try to keep my reviews away from the graphic stuff.. but I’ve decided that I’m not going to hide certain things that I really like!
