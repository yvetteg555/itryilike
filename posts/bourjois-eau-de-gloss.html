Bourjois – Eau de Gloss

Jun 12, 2008

Eau de Gloss is a high water content gloss.. moisturizing, and shiny but not sticky. Its got a little menthol in it so its very slightly cooling, not any flavor to it. Is supposed to last 4 hours, but I lick my lips a lot, so it lasts less for me, i do lick a little less because its not really a lickable gloss – but it is definitely a refreshing and perfect for the summer. I like it in clear. $15 at http://www.sephora.com/
