Pepperidge Farm Brown Sugar Cinnamon Swirl Bread

June 06, 2008

Yummy. Cinnamon, brown sugar.. simple – toast it, eat it. If you want to indulge put some butter on it. If you really want to indulge, try it with a little pat of Lescure butter – a review of this butter is on its way. Its not sugary, a good mix of cinnamon and sweetness, all is a whole grain bread. My favorite breakfast/dessert bread. (Couldnt find a pic of the brown sugar version, so I am including the cinnamon swirl pic so you atleast know what to look for)
