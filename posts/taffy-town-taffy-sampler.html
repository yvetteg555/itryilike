Taffy Town – Taffy Sampler

Oct 17, 2026

By far the best taffy you will ever try. I’ve had taffy from all the famous saltwater boardwalk east coast taffy makers, and Taffy Town blows them all away. The sampler pack is around $11 and comes with a cute little menu reminiscent of Jelly Belly’s on the back, and the flavors are spot on.

The taffy is much more milky and creamy than a lot of saltwater taffy I’ve tried, and they really do work hard to keep their flavor list on point with the trends. Additionally, it won’t pull out your teeth trying to eat it.

This has been on my birthday favor list for 2 years now, and for gifts when people come for dinner. Everyone seems to love them as much as I do.
