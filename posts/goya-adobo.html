Goya Adobo

November 14, 2008

If you have a kitchen, with food in it- then you should have a bottle of Goya Adobo seasoning in your spice cabinet. Its that important.. Adobo seasoning is a mixture of garlic oregano salt and pepper.. Its best on fish or chicken, or even red meats- and you can use it before or after you cook. It has a nice salty flavor but its perfectly balanced. I put it on everything.
