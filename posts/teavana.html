Teavana

January 30, 2011

I had passed Teavana a couple of times at the mall without going in, it was crowded, and I have so many teas already, but after realizing how many free samples they were giving away, I had to give em a try.
The whole concept is blended teas, that are mixed together to create these delicious varieties. I tried Youthberry, which was a blend of white tea and huge candied berries and fruit pieces, it was blended with Orange blossom and together With some German rock sugar it was this beautifully sweet and fruity drink. I walked away with $47 worth, p.s this stuff is expensive!
