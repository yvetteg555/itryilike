Mrs Meyers Clean Day Lavender Dish Soap

June 08, 2008

You have to do dishes, so you might as well have the lovely smell of lavender wafting up as you clean. The scent is beautiful, like sugared lavender – and it cuts through the grease. The ingredients are natural, and the bottle is cute – its got something called Soap Bark Extract, a natural degreasing agent, and essential oils – so its very fragrant. It doesnt leave your dishes smelling though, which is good – you dont want your food to smell lavendery. 4.99 at http://www.mrsmeyers.com/
