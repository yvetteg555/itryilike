Kleenex Cool Touch

December 05, 2011

Im a sneezing machine. Allergies – I have them all year long.
Normally, I buy the tissue with the lotion in it. And it helps to keep my nose from chafing – but I recently bought Kleenexs new Cool Touch tissues, and Im amazed. The tissues feel like they came out of the fridge. Not sure how they do it, because the tissue actually feels cold on your face. Their website says its a special blend of cooling ingredients, me personally, I think its magic.
