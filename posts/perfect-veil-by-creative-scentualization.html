Perfect Veil – By Creative Scentualization

May 22, 2008

Day 3 and I am trying Perfect Veil by Creative Scentualization. The initial smell was musky, and the drydown was very baby powdery but with a soapy bit to it. The notes listed on http://www.luckyscent.com/ are top: lemon, bergamot base: musk, vanilla, sandalwood

I felt like this smelled a bit like a lot of mothers smelled in the 80s, thats really the best way I can describe it. It was too powdery for me. This would be a nice gift for Moms who when they were younger loved Baby soft musk.. it is sort of like that, but much more expensive.
