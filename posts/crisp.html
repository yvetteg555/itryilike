Crisp

Jun 07, 2008

The name of the restaurant is Crisp, in midtown manhattan. Just found out about this place today, pretty catchy concept.. Handbag sandwiches, cute packaging.. And more importantly, good hummus and falafel. I ordered the Hummus Bowl with warmed chickpeas and a whole wheat pita.. Two words divine divine. The hummus was absolutely fantastic, the chickpeas were amazing, and the herbs were fresh, to make it all even more worthwhile, the pita was so fluffy and perfect (not dry, but puffy and moist) that I was tearing this thing up into the hummus. Yum yum. Claudine got a sandwich which she also enjoyed, but I will definitely be back to try the rest of the menu.
http://www.eatatcrisp.com
