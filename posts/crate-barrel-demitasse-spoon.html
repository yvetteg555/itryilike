Crate & Barrel Demitasse Spoon

June 11, 2008

I have always had a fondness for spoons. I dunno what it is. As a child, I remember asking my mom that she leave me our spoons when she died. (I was too young to realize that wasnt a nice thing to ask) – So back to the spoons – I like these because they are perfect for eating my desserts (in little baby size portions – i feel like Im eating more) – they are 4.95 for a single spoon, get a couple. They are also perfect to add to a foodie condiment gift basket, or for serving your sugar for tea.. or really any other condiment.. at Crate & Barrel www.crateandbarrel.com
