Guava gerber baby dessert

June 02, 2008

Yep I eat it. And im 30. This is my favorite thing to eat when I need something sweet but don’t want to feel horrible about it afterward.. Its got that grainy guava consistency and tastes like.. Guava!
Get a baby spoon so it lasts longer.. Or buy it and blend it was some captain morgans rum, ice and oj for a little exotic baby drink.
