Ananas Fizz

May 26, 2008

I couldnt bring myself to stop wearing the Loukhoum the other day.. I really wanted to continue wearing it today, but I knew I had more scents to try so I MUST GO ON!

Today is Ananas Fizz by L’Artisan Parfumeur – Ananas Fizz notes: pineapple, citrus essence (lemon rind, bitter orange, bergamot and pink grapefruit), pure rum, cedar, precious woods, cocoa milk, lychee sap, vanilla

At first I put this on and I felt like I smelled of some cheap fruit scented body lotion.. Very citrusy at first, then you smell the cedar, and its a little too woodsy to go with the citrus.. It doesnt smell right with my chemistry, it smells like a mix of too many scents. I smell a hint of the lychee but no cocoa milk or vanilla comes out on me at all.. With all the notes, I thought this would be somewhat gourmand, but the cedar makes it (to me) much more overpoweringly woodsy! I didnt like this one at all. I think I will try and wash it off.
