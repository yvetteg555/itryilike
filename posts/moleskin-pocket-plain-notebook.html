Moleskin Pocket Plain Notebook

Feb 02, 2009

If you’ve ever wanted to keep a journal, or a little notebook in your purse – my favorite is the Moleskin Pocket Plain Notebook Hardcover. Its the perfect place to jot down ideas, or notes, or poems. You get the idea.. Just writing in it makes you feel as important as some of its more notable users like Van Gogh or Matisse.. Its got a little elastic strap which holds the cover in place, and my favorite thing is the pocket which can hold little scraps of papers. Very handy! http://www.moleskinus.com/
