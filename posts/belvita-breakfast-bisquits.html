Belvita Breakfast Bisquits

March 04, 2012

I don’t always eat breakfast, but I know its important. I’m a commuter who doesn’t wake up early so I need something easy. I like these new Belvita breakfast bisquits because 1. They are easy 2. They are really good. 3. I feel like unlike those silly breakfast bars these things actually fill you up. They come in apple cinnamon, blueberry and golden oat. If you like your breakfast on the sweet side, try blueberry.. the other versions are much less sweet but very tasty. They are thick enough to dunk in your coffee but I’m not a believer in dunking anything. Thats just nonsense to me
