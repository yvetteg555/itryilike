International Delight – Vanilla Heat

November 12, 2013

These people at International Delight, they are innovators. Coffee innovators. Today I tried one of their newest flavors, Vanilla Heat. Its vanilla, its spicy, its a perfect way to get your face all heated up on a bitter cold morning. Why hasn’t anyone thought of this? I want it every morning. In every cup of coffee I make. This is big.
