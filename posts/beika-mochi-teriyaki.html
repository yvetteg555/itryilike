Beika Mochi – Teriyaki

Oct 17, 2026

Really amazing snack — it’s salty and sweet, crunchy throughout, but the sauce layer on the outside is just superb. Along with the big sugar crystals, it’s perfectly balanced.

Sticks are big (the length of my finger), and I’ll bring 3 to snack on while I read — the perfect size to not have to keep reaching for more. Hard to find, but on occasion Costco will have them.

One of my all time snack faves of 2026.
