Lotus

May 29, 2008

I just put this one on and at first smell, i hate it. On the initial dab, it smelled of chemical hair relaxer, as it dried down it smells.. like.. wow, cant even identify it.. i get a mix of the drycleaners, industrial floral scented cleaner, rosewater and.. gummy bears?

Lotus notes: Egyptian lotus flower extract, hints of vanilla musks

Unfortunately, Im not a florals type so Im not really sure why i tried this one.. I keep sniffing at my wrists to see if it is getting any better, and it does seem a lot fresher, but still – rose.. yucky on me..
