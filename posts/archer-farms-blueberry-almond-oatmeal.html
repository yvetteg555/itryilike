Archer Farms Blueberry Almond Oatmeal

June 05, 2008

This is the best tasting oatmeal that I have had in a long time. Sold exclusively at Target, my all time favorite place to buy anything and almost everything.. This oatmeal is more homemade tasting than your typical store bought brands.. Its got loads of little dried blueberries and almonds.. Its nice and chunky, not too sweet and only 160 calories.. Organic ingredients, and doesn’t taste all artificial and processed like quakers instant oatmeal. If you are lucky enough to have a fully stocked grocery section at target, then pick up a box and thank me later.
