Pingg.com

May 11, 2011

Just discovered pingg.com – really cute free customizable ecards, with an option to snail mail your cards and manage your invitees!
So many templates to choose from, there is an invite for anything you can have a party for. For me, its Sunday Brunch! Cute huh?
