Natures Plus – Vegetarian Fruitein

May 30, 2011

My husband drinks a lot of protein shakes. Most of them taste pretty horrible. I picked up a can of Natures Plus Vegetarian Fruitein – in Exotic Red Fruit. Its loaded with red fruits and vegetables – and when mixed with milk (I mix it with So Delicious Coconut Milk) it tastes absolutely delicious. Like a fruit smoothie. Check the ingredients list – its got every red fruit you could want (Pomegranate, Goji, Noni, Acai, Raspberry, Strawberry, and even some none red fruits like apricot, pineapple, pear and guava. I feel like its a girly protein shake, although my hubby likes it too.
