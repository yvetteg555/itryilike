Royal Red Robin Burger

June 05, 2008

Most of us like burgers, and we know they aren’t healthy.. But we indulge in them anyway. I think that if you are going to blow a diet for the day on a burger, you should make sure that it is a buger that will satisfy every molecule of your being. Im talking about the very delicious, Royal Red Robin Burger.. What makes it so good, you ask? A fried egg. (and the three strips o’ bacon doesn’t hurt either)..
Its got all that and american cheese, lettuce, tomatoes and mayo.. Mm hmm. Heaven. Wish I had a pic, looks like ill have to go get one and take its picture for you.
