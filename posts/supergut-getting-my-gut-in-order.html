Supergut – getting my gut in order

May 18, 2024

It took about two months to arrive, but I think it was worth the wait. I’ve never been regular and I despise choking down thick fiber drinks so this was a pleasant surprise. It tastes a little oatey, not unpleasant in a glass of water. Curbs your appetite and helps improve your gut. I appreciate the little packs so I can throw one in my bag to take to work, convenient. It’s too early to tell if this has revolutionized by insides but in the first week I am definitely feeling more “normal”. Stay tuned.
