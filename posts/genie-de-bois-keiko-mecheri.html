Genie de Bois – Keiko Mecheri

June 12, 2008

spicy cedary cinnamon violet – exotic and sexy. Keiko puts out so many unique and fantastic scents, I’m adding this to my list of favorites. The cedar makes it very vampy. Its got a bit of powder to it too, which I love.. from http://www.luckyscent.com/

the notes are: violet, cedar, exotic woods – it makes you feel very feminine and ruthless. Like something Lara Croft would wear when she got dressed up.. sorry, its the only thing I could think of for the description. Marvelous.
