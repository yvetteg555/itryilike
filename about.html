About iTryiLike

iTryiLike is a collection of honest micro-reviews — stuff I've tried and think you should try too, plus the occasional warning about stuff you should skip.

No sponsored posts, no fluff. If something's here, it's because I bought it, tasted it, used it, or dragged my family along to try it.

Reviews run from 2008 to 2024 — some products and packaging have changed since, so take the older ones with a grain of salt.
